# Bounty Submission Video Script 🎥

To secure a **10/10 score**, you should record a short (2-3 min) video demonstrating the UX. Here is a script designed to wow the judges.

## Introduction (0:00 - 0:30)
"Hey everyone! This is the Lazorkit Solana Starter. The goal of this project is simple: to show how we can achieve **10x better Web3 UX** using Passkeys and Smart Wallets. No seed phrases, no complex gas management."

## Part 1: Passkey Onboarding (0:30 - 1:00)
"Watch how I connect my wallet. Instead of a seed phrase or a browser extension, I just click 'Connect'. My browser triggers FaceID/TouchID... and just like that, I have a non-custodial Smart Wallet on Solana."

## Part 2: Gasless Transactions (1:00 - 1:45)
"Now, let's look at the Gasless Transfer tab. Notice my balance is small, but I can still send a transaction. Lazorkit sponsors the gas fee via a Paymaster. I sign via my Passkey, and the transaction is submitted. No SOL gas required from the user."

## Part 3: Advanced Features (1:45 - 2:30)
"We've also included a **Token Swap** demo, a **Subscription Service** for recurring billing, and a **Pay with Solana** widget for e-commerce. All of these features are powered by the same seedless, gasless architecture."

## Part 4: Developer Experience (2:30 - End)
"For developers, this repo is a modular goldmine. We've included detailed tutorials, Mermaid architecture diagrams, and high-performance React components. It's the ultimate base for your next production-ready dApp. Check it out on GitHub!"
